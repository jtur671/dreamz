# Social Media Posts: Common Dream Meanings Series

> Series concept: "Your dreams are trying to tell you something." Each post covers one of the three most universally experienced dreams, giving a mystical interpretation that drives curiosity and app downloads.

---

## 1. TikTok: Falling Dreams

**Format:** Short-form video, 30-40 seconds
**Content Pillar:** Dream Meanings / Relatable Dream Content
**Trending Sound Suggestion:** A slowed, ethereal remix or ambient mystical sound (e.g., slowed "Running Up That Hill" or trending witchtok audio)

### Script

```
HOOK (0-2s):
[Text overlay: "You jolt awake mid-fall."]
[Creator speaks to camera, dark moody lighting, maybe fairy lights or candles in background]
"If you've ever had a falling dream, your subconscious is literally screaming at you."

BODY (2-20s):
[Cut to b-roll: slow-mo footage of clouds/sky from below, or dark starry visuals]
[Text overlays appear with each point]

"Falling dreams are one of the most common dreams on the planet,
and no -- it doesn't mean something bad is coming."

[Back to creator]
"In dream symbolism, falling often reflects a moment where you
feel like you're losing control -- maybe at work, in a relationship,
or just... life in general."

"But here's the part no one talks about:"

PAYOFF (20-30s):
[Lean in, lower voice, conspiratorial tone]
"The fall isn't the message. It's what you do IN the fall.
Did you panic? Did you surrender? Did you fly?"

[Text overlay: "Your reaction IS the reading."]

"That's what separates a dream dictionary from an actual reading."

CTA (30-40s):
[Show phone with Dreamz app open, dark UI visible]
"I've been logging mine in this app called Dreamz -- it gives you
a full mystical reading with symbols, omens, even a ritual.
It's like having a tarot reader for your subconscious."

[Text overlay: "Dreamz -- link in bio"]
"Drop your falling dream in the comments. I'll tell you what
your reaction means."
```

### Visual Direction
- Dark, moody lighting throughout. Candles, fairy lights, or purple/blue LED ambient light.
- Creator wears dark, cozy, witchy-adjacent aesthetic (black, layered, maybe crystals).
- B-roll: clouds from below, starry sky timelapses, slow-motion fabric falling.
- App showcase: brief, organic, 3-4 seconds max. Show the reading screen.
- Text overlays: clean serif or mystical font, white or gold on dark background.

### Hashtags
```
#dreammeaning #fallingdream #dreaminterpretation #witchtok #spiritualtiktok #dreamjournal #dreamz #subconsciousmind #dreamsymbolism #spiritualawakening
```

### Notes
- Encourage comments by asking about their specific falling dream experience.
- Pin a comment: "Falling + feeling peaceful = you're learning to let go. Falling + terror = something in your waking life feels unstable. Which one are you?"
- Reply to comments with mini-readings to drive engagement.

## 2. Instagram Carousel: Teeth Falling Out Dreams

**Format:** Carousel, 5 slides, 1080x1350 (portrait)
**Content Pillar:** Dream Meanings (educational, shareable)
**Posting Time:** Tuesday or Thursday, 11am-1pm or 7-9pm

### Slide Content

**SLIDE 1 -- The Hook**
- **Visual:** Dark midnight blue background with a subtle celestial pattern. Center illustration of ethereal teeth dissolving into golden stardust. Ornate gold border detail.
- **Text:** "Your teeth fell out in a dream?"
- **Subtext:** "Here's what the veil is showing you."

**SLIDE 2 -- The Common Interpretation**
- **Visual:** Deep purple background, small crescent moon icon at top. Clean layout.
- **Text headline:** "The surface reading"
- **Body text:**
  - "Teeth dreams often surface during moments of transition."
  - "They may reflect anxiety about appearance, fear of judgment, or feeling powerless in a situation you can't control."
- **Design note:** Use a subtle tooth-to-moon visual metaphor.

**SLIDE 3 -- The Deeper Layer**
- **Visual:** Darker gradient, gold accent lines, a single ornate eye symbol.
- **Text headline:** "But go deeper."
- **Body text:**
  - "In mystical dream work, teeth represent your ability to 'bite into' life -- your confidence, your voice, your personal power."
  - "Losing them may suggest something is silencing you. What aren't you saying?"
- **Design note:** This is the insight slide -- the one people screenshot and share.

**SLIDE 4 -- The Ritual**
- **Visual:** Warm gold tones on dark background. Small candle or flame illustration.
- **Text headline:** "A ritual for after this dream"
- **Body text:**
  - "Write down the one thing you've been holding back from saying."
  - "Read it aloud to yourself -- even whispered."
  - "Then burn or bury the paper. Release it."
- **Design note:** Actionable and shareable. This drives saves.

**SLIDE 5 -- The CTA**
- **Visual:** Dreamz app UI screenshot (reading screen) on dark starry background. App icon visible.
- **Text:** "Get your full dream reading."
- **Subtext:** "Dreamz gives you symbols, omens, rituals, and journal prompts for every dream. Your personal grimoire."
- **Button-style text:** "Free on the App Store"

```

Sound familiar? You're not alone -- teeth dreams are one of the top 3 most common dreams worldwide.

But here's what most dream dictionaries won't tell you: the meaning changes depending on YOUR life right now. A teeth dream during a breakup reads completely differently than one before a big presentation.

That's why a static definition isn't enough. You need a reading.

Swipe through for the deeper meaning + a ritual to try tonight.

Save this for the next time you wake up shaken.

#dreammeaning #teethfallingout #dreaminterpretation #witchesofinstagram #dreamjournal #spirituality #dreamz #mysticalliving #dreamwork #shadowwork
```

### Visual Direction Summary
- Color palette: deep midnight blue, dark purple, warm gold accents.
- Typography: primary headlines in an elegant serif (like Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond), body in clean sans-serif.
- Consistent ornate border or celestial frame across all slides.
- Illustrations over photos: ethereal, mystical digital art style (tarot-card inspired).
- Final slide uses an actual app screenshot composited onto the dark background.

## Series Notes

**Cross-promotion strategy:**
- TikTok drives awareness and comments (falling dream).
- Instagram carousel drives saves and shares (teeth dream).
- Pinterest drives evergreen search traffic (chased dream).
- All three link back to the App Store listing.
- Repurpose: The TikTok script can be adapted as an Instagram Reel. The carousel slides can be reformatted as individual Pinterest pins. The Pinterest description copy can seed blog/landing page SEO content.

**Suggested posting cadence:**
- Post one per week across platforms as a "Common Dreams Decoded" mini-series.
- Week 1: TikTok (falling) -- drives initial buzz and comments.
- Week 2: Instagram carousel (teeth) -- capitalizes on followers gained from TikTok.
- Week 3: Pinterest pin (chased) -- plants evergreen search content.
- Week 4: Recap Reel combining all three with "Which one do you get?" poll in Stories.

- Flying dreams
