### Notes

- Variant A leads with the emotional hook (dreams are speaking) and positions the app as a translator between worlds. Slightly more poetic.
- Variant B leads with urgency (dreams fade) and leans harder into the feature set. More concrete, slightly higher information density.
- Both avoid exclamation points, clinical language, and certainty claims per brand voice guidelines.
- Recommended A/B test: run both against the same audience segment and measure scroll depth + CTA click rate.
