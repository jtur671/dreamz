# Welcome Email — New User Download

> Trigger: User creates account (first app open + signup)
> Send timing: Immediately after signup
> Funnel stage: Onboarding / Retention
> One CTA: Open the app and record first dream

---

**Subject Line:**
The veil is thinner than you think

**Preview Text:**
Your grimoire is ready. Your first reading is waiting.

---

**Body:**

Welcome to Dreamz.

Tonight — or maybe tomorrow morning, half-awake with images still dissolving — you will have something worth writing down. A place you have never been. A person whose face keeps shifting. Water, doors, falling, flight. Whatever arrives, Dreamz is ready to receive it.

Here is how it works. Write your dream or record it with your voice before the details fade. Dreamz will return a full mystical reading: the symbols hidden in your narrative, the omen your dream may carry, a ritual to ground what you have seen, and a journal prompt to help you sit with it longer. This is not a dictionary lookup. It is a personal reading drawn from the specific landscape of your dream.

Your dreams are encrypted and private. We do not read them, share them, or train on them. Your grimoire belongs to you — exportable, deletable, always under your control. The only person who sees what moves through your sleep is you.

Your readings are unlimited. Use the first one tonight.

---

**CTA Button:**
Open Dreamz

---

### Notes

- Subject line uses "the veil" language from brand keyword list — atmospheric without being clickbait.
- Preview text reinforces product vocabulary (grimoire, reading) to attract the target persona in inbox scan.
- Email follows Hook (mystery) -> Value (how it works) -> Trust (privacy) -> CTA (use it tonight) structure.
- Privacy paragraph is intentionally placed before the CTA to reduce friction — the target persona is privacy-conscious.
- Single CTA per email as per brand guidelines.
- No exclamation points. No certainty claims. Interpretation framing maintained ("may carry").
