# Video 01: Falling Dreams — Production Guide

> "If you've ever had a falling dream, your subconscious is literally screaming at you."

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 30-35 seconds
**Style:** AI-generated character + atmospheric b-roll
**Source script:** `marketing/content/social-posts-common-dreams.md` (TikTok section)

---

## Segment Breakdown

### Segment A: Hook (0-3s)
- **Tool:** Hedra (TTS + character image)
- **Script:** "If you've ever had a falling dream, your subconscious is literally screaming at you."
- **Text overlay:** "You jolt awake mid-fall." (white or gold serif, center screen)
- **Notes:** This is the make-or-break moment. The text overlay appears immediately while the character starts speaking.

### Segment B: B-roll Transition (3-6s)
- **Tool:** Veo3
- **Visual:** Slow-motion POV falling through dark purple storm clouds with stars
- **Text overlay:** None (let the visual breathe)
- **Notes:** This separates the hook from the explanation. Gives viewers a beat.

### Segment C: Explanation (6-16s)
- **Tool:** Hedra (TTS + character image)
- **Script:** "Falling dreams are one of the most common dreams on the planet. And no, it doesn't mean something bad is coming. In dream symbolism, falling often reflects a moment where you feel like you're losing control. Maybe at work, in a relationship, or just... life in general."
- **Text overlay:** None (let captions handle it)

### Segment D: Pivot + Payoff (16-25s)
- **Tool:** Hedra (TTS + character image)
- **Script:** "But here's the part no one talks about. The fall isn't the message. It's what you do in the fall. Did you panic? Did you surrender? Did you fly? Your reaction is the reading. That's what separates a dream dictionary from an actual reading."
- **Text overlay:** "Your reaction IS the reading." (gold, appears at the key moment)

### Segment E: CTA (25-32s)
- **Tool:** Static image / screen recording
- **Visual:** App reading screen (`store/generated/03-reading.png`) on dark starry background
- **Voiceover (optional):** "I've been using this app called Dreamz. It gives you a full mystical reading with symbols, omens, even a ritual. Link in bio."
- **Text overlay:** "Dreamz — link in bio" (gold, bottom third)
- **Notes:** If skipping voiceover, use text-only CTA with the app screenshot. Either works.

## Character Image

Generate ONE character image to use as the Hedra source for all speaking segments (A, C, D).

See `ai-prompts.md` for the exact prompt. Save the generated image — you'll upload it to Hedra for each segment.

---

## Assembly (CapCut)

1. **New project** — 9:16 (1080x1920)
2. **Import** all clips: Hedra segments (A, C, D), Veo3 b-roll (B), app screenshot (E)
3. **Arrange** on timeline in order: A → B → C → D → E
4. **Text overlays:**
   - 0-3s: "You jolt awake mid-fall." — white or gold (#c9a84c), serif font, center
   - 18-22s: "Your reaction IS the reading." — gold, center, slightly larger
   - 25-32s: "Dreamz — link in bio" — gold, lower third
5. **Auto-captions:** Enable (CapCut > Text > Auto Captions). Style them white on dark, clean sans-serif
6. **Music:** Search CapCut library for "ethereal", "ambient", "mystical" — keep volume at ~20% under voice
7. **Color grading:** If needed, push shadows toward purple/blue. The Hedra + Veo3 outputs may already match.
8. **Transitions:** Simple crossfade between segments (0.3-0.5s). No flashy effects.
9. **Export:** MP4, 1080x1920, 30fps

**Target length:** 30-35 seconds. If it runs long, tighten Segment C (explanation) first.
