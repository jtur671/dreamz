# Video 01: Falling Dreams — AI Prompts

> Copy-paste these into each tool. All prompts are ready to use.

---

## 1. Character Image (MCP Image Generation)

Generate this FIRST. The output becomes your Hedra source image for all speaking segments.

```
Portrait photo of a young woman in her mid-20s with a mystical, witchy aesthetic. She has dark wavy hair, warm brown eyes, and subtle gold jewelry (thin chain necklace, small hoop earrings). She is sitting in a dimly lit room with candles and fairy lights in the background creating soft bokeh. The lighting is moody with deep purple and warm amber tones. She wears a dark, cozy oversized sweater. Her expression is knowing and slightly conspiratorial, like she's about to share a secret. Shot from chest up, looking directly at camera, shallow depth of field. The overall mood is intimate, mystical, and inviting — like a wise friend reading tarot in her apartment.
```

**Save the output image** — you'll upload this to Hedra for each speaking segment.

---

## 2. Hedra Speaking Segments

Upload the character image from Step 1 to Hedra. Use TTS (built-in text-to-speech). Generate each segment separately.

### Segment A: Hook (target ~3 seconds)

**Script to paste into Hedra:**
```
If you've ever had a falling dream, your subconscious is literally screaming at you.
```

**Settings:** Female voice, natural/warm tone. Normal pace — slightly dramatic.

---

### Segment C: Explanation (target ~10 seconds)

**Script to paste into Hedra:**
```
Falling dreams are one of the most common dreams on the planet. And no, it doesn't mean something bad is coming. In dream symbolism, falling often reflects a moment where you feel like you're losing control. Maybe at work, in a relationship, or just... life in general.
```

**Settings:** Same voice as Segment A. Conversational, steady pace.

---

### Segment D: Pivot + Payoff (target ~9 seconds)

**Script to paste into Hedra:**
```
But here's the part no one talks about. The fall isn't the message. It's what you do in the fall. Did you panic? Did you surrender? Did you fly? Your reaction is the reading. That's what separates a dream dictionary from an actual reading.
```

**Settings:** Same voice. Starts quieter/conspiratorial on "But here's the part no one talks about", then builds energy through the questions, lands with conviction on "That's what separates a dream dictionary from an actual reading."

---

### Segment E: CTA Voiceover (optional, target ~7 seconds)

**Script to paste into Hedra:**
```
I've been using this app called Dreamz. It gives you a full mystical reading with symbols, omens, even a ritual. Link in bio.
```

**Settings:** Same voice. Casual, recommending-to-a-friend energy. If you skip this, use text-only CTA over the app screenshot instead.

## 3. Veo3 B-roll Clips

Generate 2 clips. Use whichever works best for Segment B (3-6 seconds).

### B-roll Option 1: Falling Through Clouds

```
POV shot falling in slow motion through dark purple storm clouds at night. Stars are faintly visible between the clouds. Deep midnight blue and violet color palette with occasional golden light breaking through gaps in the clouds. Cinematic, atmospheric, dreamlike quality. Camera tumbles gently. 4 seconds, vertical 9:16 format.
```

### B-roll Option 2: Starry Sky

```
Slow upward camera movement through a dark night sky filled with stars. Golden constellation lines slowly trace between stars, connecting them into patterns. Deep purple and midnight blue gradient sky. Ethereal, mystical atmosphere. Gentle particle effects like golden dust drifting. 4 seconds, vertical 9:16 format.
```

### B-roll Option 3 (backup): Abstract Falling

```
Abstract slow motion shot of dark translucent fabric falling through a void with golden particles floating around it. Deep purple and black color palette with warm amber light sources. Dreamlike, surreal, atmospheric. The fabric billows and catches light as it descends. 4 seconds, vertical 9:16 format.
```
