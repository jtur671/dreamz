## After Posting: Engagement Plan

For the first 48 hours, reply to EVERY comment with a mini dream reading. This is critical for algorithm momentum.

**Example replies:**

> Comment: "I always fall and then jolt awake right before I hit the ground"
> Reply: "That jolt is your nervous system pulling you back — but the fact that you never land? That often means you're avoiding confronting something. What's the thing you keep almost dealing with but pulling away from? That's your reading."

> Comment: "I fall but then I start flying"
> Reply: "That's a powerful shift — falling into flying often surfaces when you're going through something scary but you're starting to trust yourself through it. Your subconscious is showing you that you already have what you need."

> Comment: "I've had this dream like 100 times"
> Reply: "Recurring falling dreams are your subconscious being persistent. It's the same message on repeat because something in your waking life hasn't shifted yet. What area of your life feels the most unstable right now? Start there."

**Tone:** mystical but conversational. Like a wise friend, not a therapist. Use "often suggests", "may reflect" — never certainty claims.
