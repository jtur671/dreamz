Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 9, 2026

Introduction

Dreamz ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile dream journal application that uses artificial intelligence to generate mystical interpretations of your dreams. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store it, and what rights you have over your data. By using Dreamz, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We believe your dreams are deeply personal. Our guiding principle is simple: your data belongs to you, and we collect only what is necessary to provide the service.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Email address. Required for account creation and authentication. This is the only piece of personally identifiable information we require.
  • Dream text entries. The content you write describing your dreams.
  • Optional profile details. You may optionally provide your zodiac sign, gender, and age range to receive more personalized dream readings. None of these fields are required.

Information Generated by the Service

  • Dream readings. AI-generated interpretations of your dream entries.
  • Subscription status. Whether you are on the free or premium tier.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Precise location or GPS data
  • Exact date of birth
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Browsing history or activity outside the app
  • We do not use first-party analytics services or tracking pixels (third-party ad SDKs may collect data as described below)

Advertising

Dreamz displays interstitial ads to users on the free tier via Google AdMob. These ads appear between dream readings. Premium subscribers do not see ads. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or AAID on Android), device information, and app interaction data in accordance with Google's privacy policy. We do not use your dream content, email address, or profile data for ad targeting.

You can limit ad tracking through your device settings: on iOS, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and operate the Dreamz service:

  • Dream analysis. Your dream text is sent to the OpenAI API to generate AI-powered dream interpretations. Only the dream text is sent; your email and other PII are not included.
  • Personalized readings. If you provide your zodiac sign, gender, or age range, this information tailors your readings but is not linked to your identity when sent to OpenAI.
  • Authentication. Your email address secures your account.
  • Subscription management. Your subscription status determines available features.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with data brokers or any third party for marketing purposes. Limited technical identifiers (device advertising IDs) may be shared with ad networks for ad delivery as described above. We do not use your data to train AI models. We do not send marketing emails.

AI Consent and Control

Dreamz uses artificial intelligence to generate dream interpretations. Before any dream text is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI), you must explicitly grant consent through an in-app toggle found in Settings.

  • Consent is optional. You can use Dreamz as a private dream journal without enabling AI readings.
  • Consent is revocable. You can withdraw consent at any time from Settings. Once withdrawn, no new dream text will be sent to AI services. Previously generated readings remain in your account unless you delete them.
  • What is sent. When consent is granted and you request a reading, only the dream text and optional profile details (zodiac sign, gender, age range) are sent to OpenAI. No email address or other personally identifiable information is included.
  • We record your consent. We store whether you have granted AI consent and the date you granted it, so we can honor your preference and demonstrate compliance.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database with encryption at rest. We protect your data with:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring each user can only access their own data
  • JWT authentication for all requests
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all communication
  • No sensitive data in logs

Your Rights and Choices

  • Export your data as JSON from the Settings screen at any time.
  • Delete individual dreams. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone from the app.
  • Delete your account and all data permanently from Settings.
  • Update or remove optional profile details at any time.
  • Control AI processing. Grant or withdraw consent for AI dream analysis at any time from Settings.

Data Retention

  • Active accounts: data retained while account is active.
  • Deleted dreams: removed from your journal immediately and permanently purged from our database within 30 days.
  • Deleted accounts: all data permanently removed.
  • Inactive accounts: may be deleted after 12 months with advance notice.

Children's Privacy

Dreamz is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or via email.
