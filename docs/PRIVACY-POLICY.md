# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 18, 2026

## Introduction

Dreamz ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile dream journal application that uses artificial intelligence to generate mystical interpretations of your dreams. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store it, and what rights you have over your data. By using Dreamz, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We believe your dreams are deeply personal. Our guiding principle is simple: your data belongs to you, and we collect only what is necessary to provide the service.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Email address.** Required for account creation and authentication. This is the only piece of personally identifiable information we require.
- **Dream text entries.** The content you write describing your dreams. This is user-submitted content that you choose to enter into the app.
- **Optional profile details.** You may optionally provide your zodiac sign, gender, and age range to receive more personalized dream readings. None of these fields are required.

### Information Generated by the Service

- **Dream readings.** AI-generated interpretations of your dream entries, including symbols, omens, rituals, and journal prompts.
- **Subscription status.** Whether you are on the free or premium tier, managed through Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and RevenueCat.

### Information We Do Not Collect

- Precise location or GPS data
- Exact date of birth
- Contacts or address book data
- Device identifiers for advertising purposes
- Browsing history or activity outside the app
- We do not use analytics services, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs
- We do not serve ads of any kind

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and operate the Dreamz service:

- **Dream analysis.** Your dream text is sent to the OpenAI API to generate AI-powered dream interpretations. Only the dream text is sent; your email address and other personally identifiable information are not included in these API requests.
- **Personalized readings.** If you choose to provide your zodiac sign, gender, or age range, this information is used to tailor the tone and content of your dream readings. These details are included in the AI prompt context but are not linked to your identity when sent to OpenAI.
- **Authentication.** Your email address is used to create and secure your account.
- **Subscription management.** Your subscription status is tracked to determine which features are available to you.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for marketing purposes. We do not use your data to train AI models. We do not send marketing emails.

## Data Storage and Security

### Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database located in the United States. Supabase provides encryption at rest for all stored data.

### How We Protect Your Data

- **Row Level Security (RLS).** Every database table enforces row-level security policies, ensuring that each user can only access their own data. No user can view, modify, or delete another user's dreams or profile.
- **JWT authentication.** All authenticated requests use JSON Web Tokens to verify user identity.
- **HTTPS encryption.** All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
- **No sensitive data in logs.** Dream content, email addresses, and other personal information are excluded from server logs.

### Data in Transit to Third Parties

When your dream text is sent to OpenAI for analysis, it is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection. We send only the dream text and optional profile context (zodiac sign, gender, age range) -- never your email address, account ID, or other identifying information.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data within Dreamz:

- **Export your data.** You can export all of your data (dreams, readings, and profile information) as a JSON file directly from the app's Settings screen at any time.
- **Delete individual dreams.** You can delete any dream entry from your journal. Deleted dreams enter a 30-day soft-delete recovery period, during which you can restore them. After 30 days, they are permanently and irreversibly deleted.
- **Delete your account.** You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated data from the Settings screen. This action removes your profile, all dream entries, all readings, and any other data linked to your account. Account deletion is irreversible.
- **Update your profile.** You can change or remove your optional profile details (zodiac sign, gender, age range) at any time from the Settings screen.
- **Withhold optional information.** All profile fields beyond your email address are optional. The app functions without them; readings will simply be less personalized.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction that grants additional data protection rights, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, or restriction of processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address listed below.

## Data Retention

- **Active accounts.** Your data is retained for as long as your account is active and you continue to use the service.
- **Deleted dreams.** Individual deleted dreams are retained in a soft-delete state for 30 days to allow recovery, after which they are permanently deleted.
- **Deleted accounts.** When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems.
- **Inactive accounts.** We may delete accounts and their associated data after an extended period of inactivity (12 months or more), with advance notice sent to your registered email address.

## Children's Privacy

Dreamz is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address below so we can take appropriate action.
