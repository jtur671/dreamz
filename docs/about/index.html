What We Believe About Dreams

Dreams are personal symbolic language — not predictions, not diagnoses, and not riddles with single right answers. A snake means one thing in your psyche and something else in mine, because the symbol arrives wrapped in everything we’ve already lived. The most useful dream interpretation isn’t a lookup; it’s an invitation to ask what is this part of me trying to say in the only language it has?

That’s the lens every reading on Dreamz is built around. We hedge on purpose — “may reflect,” “often suggests” — because certainty about someone else’s dream is almost always a small lie.

The Dreamz iOS app shipped on April 15, 2026, after eight months of building, breaking, and rebuilding. It runs on a 5,700+ symbol dictionary that anchors every AI-generated reading to a consistent symbolic vocabulary, so logging the same dream twice doesn’t produce wildly different answers. Free users get one full reading a day; premium ($5.99/month or $49.99/year) unlocks 30 readings, dream imagery, and removes ads. Everything in the journal is private, encrypted, and never used to train a model.
