Common Questions

How many free readings do I get?

Free accounts get 3 dream readings per month. Your reading count resets on the 1st of each month.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your iPhone to manage or cancel.

Can I export my dreams?

Yes. Go to Settings > "Gather Your Dreams" to export all your dreams and readings as a JSON file.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > "Close the Grimoire Forever". This permanently deletes your account and all data. This cannot be undone.

Is my dream data private?

Yes. Your dreams are encrypted, protected by row-level security, and never shared with third parties for marketing. We don't serve ads or sell data.
