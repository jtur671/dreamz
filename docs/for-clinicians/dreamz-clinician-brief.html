Dreamz for Clinicians

A private, structured dream-journaling companion for clients between sessions.

What it is. Dreamz is a consumer iOS journaling app built for one purpose: to make the between-session dream journal a client will actually keep. It is not a medical device, not HIPAA-covered, and not a clinical decision-support tool. Think modern paper journal — with voice capture, tagging, a searchable timeline, and optional AI-generated reflection prompts.

Who it's for. Clinicians who already assign dream work as part of practice: Jungian and depth-psychology modalities, trauma-informed and EMDR clinicians, somatic therapists, and psychedelic-integration providers.

Three claims we can defend

Private by design.

Encrypted at rest. No model training on user content. The client owns their journal on their device — no therapist dashboard, no shared record, no cross-user analytics.

Structured homework.

Voice-to-text in under 30 seconds on waking. Tagged entries, recurring-symbol tracking, a 5,700-entry reference. Low friction means the homework actually gets done.

Recommend without a purchase.

Free tier (unlimited journaling + 1 reflection prompt/day) covers weekly homework. Premium ($5.99/mo or $49.99/yr) is optional. No affiliate or referral payments.

What we do not claim. Dreamz is not therapeutic, clinical, diagnostic, predictive, HIPAA-compliant, evidence-based for AI-assisted interpretation, or a replacement for therapy. Reflection prompts are hedged ("may suggest", "often reflects"). Distress keywords trigger grounding and crisis-line output in place of interpretation.
